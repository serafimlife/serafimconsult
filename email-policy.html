Email Outreach & Unsubscribe Policy

We send business outreach emails only to individually identified prospects, contacted directly by Serafim Ivanov — never through purchased, rented, or scraped contact lists, and never via third-party affiliates, resellers, or list brokers of any kind.

Every outreach email includes a clear way to opt out of future contact. Any opt-out or unsubscribe request is honored immediately and permanently — the contact will not be emailed again from any our address or domain.

If you believe you received an email from this domain in error, or you wish to be excluded from all future contact, email serafim@lifecyclepractice.com with "unsubscribe" in the subject line.

This policy applies to all senders using this domain, including Serafim Ivanov and any tools or services used to send email on the domain's behalf.
